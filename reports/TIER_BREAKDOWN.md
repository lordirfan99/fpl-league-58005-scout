## Scoring Methodology

Each manager is scored out of 100 based on 4 components:

### 1. Best Rank Score (0–40)
- Best rank ≤ 10,000 → 40 pts
- Best rank ≤ 50,000 → 30 pts
- Best rank ≤ 100,000 → 22 pts
- Best rank ≤ 500,000 → 15 pts
- Best rank ≤ 1,000,000 → 8 pts
- Best rank > 1,000,000 → 4 pts

### 2. Last Season Rank Score (0–30) — Recent form weighted highest
- Same thresholds as Best Rank, scaled to 30 max

### 3. Consistency Score (0–20) — Based on rank stability across seasons
- Coefficient of Variation (CV) = std_dev / mean_rank
- CV < 0.3 → 20 pts (very consistent)
- CV < 0.6 → 15 pts
- CV < 1.0 → 10 pts
- CV ≥ 1.0 → 5 pts (erratic)
- Single season only → 10 pts (insufficient data)

### 4. Experience Bonus (0–10)
- 5+ seasons → 10 pts
- 3–4 seasons → 7 pts
- 2 seasons → 5 pts
- 1 season → 3 pts
